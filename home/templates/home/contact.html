Customer Care

Jewellery Care

Keep your jewellery beautiful and shining with proper care. Follow these simple tips to maintain the beauty and quality of your precious jewellery.

  • Clean jewellery with a soft cloth after use.
  • Store each jewellery piece separately.
  • Avoid contact with perfumes and chemicals.
  • Get professional cleaning for long-lasting shine.

BIS Hallmark

BIS Hallmark ensures the purity and authenticity of gold jewellery. Always check hallmark details before purchasing gold jewellery.

  • BIS Logo confirms certification.
  • Purity mark shows gold quality.
  • HUID number provides authenticity.

Ring Size Guide

Choosing the correct ring size gives a comfortable and perfect fit.

  • Measure your finger at room temperature.
  • Avoid measuring when fingers are too cold or hot.
  • Visit our showroom for accurate sizing assistance.
